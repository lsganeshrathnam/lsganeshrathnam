## Projects

### [Heart Disease Prediction](https://github.com/lsganeshrathnam/cse572-heart-disease-prediction)
Benchmarked seven classifiers on a 900,000-record clinical dataset, tuning each
with GridSearchCV and stratified 5-fold cross-validation. The tuned AdaBoost
model reached **0.9546 AUC-ROC** on validation and 0.9535 on the Kaggle private
leaderboard. Cost-sensitive threshold optimization weighting false negatives 5x
cut missed diagnoses by **67%** and raised recall from 86.1% to 95.5%.
Per-feature ablation and Welch's t-tests on the false-negative subgroup isolated
the model's systematic blind spot.

*Team project for CSE 572 Data Mining at ASU.*

### [Student Performance Prediction](https://github.com/lsganeshrathnam/student-performance-prediction)
Benchmarked six classifiers — Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM, AdaBoost, and
two Keras neural networks (MLP and ANN) — to predict student grade categories
from socio-economic and academic features. Random Forest reached **86.6%
accuracy** and the three-class Keras network 82.8%.

### [Bangalore Housing Price Prediction](https://github.com/lsganeshrathnam/bangalore-housing-price-prediction)
Built during my data science internship at Knowledge Solutions India. Extracted,
cleaned, and validated over 35,000 real-estate records, then tuned regression
models to an **R² of 0.86** while cutting MAE by 18%. Deployed as a REST API with
inference latency under 200 ms.
